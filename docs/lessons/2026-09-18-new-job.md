# Lesson, 18 Sep 2026: talking about a new job

Cleaned from the video-call captions, which caught very little of the Greek, so much of the lesson survives only as Mike's English asides and the teacher's corrections. **[?]** marks Greek reconstructed from context, and **[…]** marks a stretch that could not be recovered. Filler, the chat before and after the lesson, and the details of the job are cut.

**0:04 · Next week**

**Teacher:** Here are three verbs, with their translations. Make sentences with them. You start a new job next week.

**Mike:** **Ξεκινάω καινούργια δουλειά την επόμενη εβδομάδα** [?]. They're a small company: **είναι μια μικρή εταιρεία** [?], **ίσως δεκαπέντε** [?] people. Am I missing an article there?

**Teacher:** No.

**4:24 · πολλούς ανθρώπους στην Αμερική**

**Mike:** They have **πολλούς ανθρώπους** [?]… Αμερική. Is there a better word, or "from"? But it's not "from", because it's "in".

**Teacher:** You need the σε.

**Mike:** I completely forgot that: **στην Αμερική**. So: **έχουν πολλούς ανθρώπους στην Αμερική** [?].

**Teacher:** Perfect.

**7:56 · πράγματα**

**Mike:** All three of those words are in the exact same box for me, and I can't remember which is which.

**Teacher:** Things: **πράγματα**.

**9:04 · μιλάω, μίλησα**

**Mike:** Μιλάνε [?]… Sorry, I was thinking of the conjugation and flipped the words.

**Teacher:** **Μιλάω**, and the past, **μίλησα** [?].

**11:28 · Boss, not bus**

**Teacher:** Tell me about your boss.

**Mike:** Where would I put "a lot" in that sentence?

**Teacher:** After the verb.

**Mike:** How do I say "how many hours"? I remember it being either "how many" or "how much".

**Teacher:** **Πόσες ώρες.** And the bus? We're talking about the bus, right? Are you going to go by bus?

**Mike:** Sorry, boss as in employer. That's my accent kicking in.

**21:36 · Lazy**

**Mike:** I want to say "lazy", but it's not fair…

**Teacher:** **Τεμπέλης** [?]. And we also have an expression, for when you want to offend someone. A donkey is stubborn.

**Mike:** In Afrikaans, a donkey is a kind of "lazy horse". I never realised that was just us.

**24:04 · A game with the body**
