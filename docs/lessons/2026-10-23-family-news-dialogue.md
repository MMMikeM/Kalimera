# Lesson, 23 Oct 2026: family news, a role-play

Cleaned from the video-call captions. They caught very little of the Greek, so most of the role-play survives only as Mike's English asides and the teacher's corrections. **[?]** marks Greek reconstructed from context, and **[…]** marks a stretch that could not be recovered. Filler and the chat before and after the lesson are cut.

The homework was a pair of role cards: two old friends meet after years and share their family news. Role A's children are married and they have two grandchildren. Role B's son is married and expecting a child, and B's daughter has a son outside marriage.

---

**5:12 · Choosing roles**

**Teacher:** So you're not escaping from this dialogue. Will you be person B first? The first time I won't be very talkative. The second time I'll be more chatty. Let's see what you do.

**Mike:** I'm happy with either. I've read through it a lot of times.

**Teacher:** Ω, Μάικ! […] Long time no see.

**Mike:** Am I supposed to be practising this dialogue, or doing something different?

**Teacher:** You say what's on your card. The speed is the speed.

**9:28 · Θα γίνεις παππούς**

**Mike:** I'm almost a grandfather. Is that what you said?

**Teacher:** Ουάου! **Θα γίνεις παππούς** [?]. You're going to be a grandfather.

**Mike:** My son grew up really quickly.

**Teacher:** Κοίτα! Α, **είσαι ήδη παππούς** [?]. You're already a grandfather.

**12:04 · Old is "big"**

**Mike:** I can't remember what "old" is.

**Teacher:** You can say "big": **μεγάλος**.

**Mike:** Is "never" right there, with the double negative? Afrikaans does matched negatives too, so I'm still not sure about it.

**Teacher:** Mhm. **Ποτέ** [?].

**13:28 · φυσικά, μακριά, σου αρέσει**

**Mike:** What's the response for "of course", "obviously"?

**Teacher:** **Φυσικά.**

**Mike:** I'm still trying to remember […]

**Teacher:** **Μακριά**, far away.

**Mike:** Αρέσει σου [?]…

**Teacher:** Backwards. **Σου αρέσει.**

**15:52 · Who lives where**

**Mike:** Are you saying they all live in the same house? I'm trying to say "nearby" or "next to", but that's wrong: it needs to be "together".

**Teacher:** **Χωριστά** [?], separately.

**17:08 · Whose child?**

**Mike:** It's going fast, I'm struggling to keep up. You're asking about my son and daughter?

**Teacher:** Your grandchild.

**Mike:** Grandchild is **εγγόνι**, and granddaughter is **εγγονή**. And grandson is **εγγονός**.

**Teacher:** Αγόρι ή κορίτσι; Is it a boy or a girl?

**Mike:** You were asking about my son's daughter, so my granddaughter…

**Teacher:** No, I said the son of your daughter. Read the last sentence on your card. How would you say "my daughter's son"?

**Mike:** Is it […], because it's feminine?

**Teacher:** No.

*The answer is **ο γιος της κόρης μου**: κόρη takes the Owner form, της κόρης.*

**21:16 · How did you prepare?**

**Teacher:** How did you prepare for this dialogue? And where did you find **να σας ζήσει**? I didn't give it to you.

**Mike:** I sent a photo of the cards to an AI to make the text legible, and it wrote a whole dialogue too. I read through all the sentences and practised them, so I'd be comfortable with them.

**Teacher:** It did the exercise for you. You could have just read it to me. When someone has a baby, we say **να σας ζήσει** as a wish. For more than one child, **να σας ζήσουν**.

**Mike:** The intention wasn't to cheat. I thought we'd talk through the sentences, as in the last lesson. **Αλήθεια** is a great word, though.

**Teacher:** You only read a dialogue, so you didn't prepare any questions.

**26:28 · Wrap-up**

**Teacher:** Take care of all your children and grandchildren.

**Mike:** Even the one out of wedlock.

---

**What the lesson produced, in correct Greek:**

- Θα γίνεις παππούς! — You're going to be a grandfather!
- Είσαι ήδη παππούς. — You're already a grandfather.
- Είναι μεγάλος. — He's old. (literally "big")
- Φυσικά. — Of course.
- Μένουν μακριά. — They live far away.
- Σου αρέσει; — Do you like it? (σου comes first)
- το εγγόνι · ο εγγονός · η εγγονή — grandchild · grandson · granddaughter
- Αγόρι ή κορίτσι; — Boy or girl?
- ο γιος της κόρης μου — my daughter's son
- Να σας ζήσει! · Να σας ζήσουν! — the wish for a new child · for more than one

**For next time:** the role cards are a prompt to improvise, not a script. Prepare the words and the questions you'll need (who is married, how old, boy or girl, where they live), then say what you can in the moment.
